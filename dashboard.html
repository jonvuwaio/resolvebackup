        How to publish just the job names
        1. Make a new Google Sheet, for example "Job names for Colour Project Manager".
        2. In A1 put =IMPORTRANGE("1pw-T6EYLZ2ls8oIuQKCWbss7usjrWrUoqgqZiZDY0wA", "CURRENT!G:G") and press Allow access.
        3. File > Share > Publish to web: choose that sheet and Comma-separated values (.csv), press Publish and paste the link here.
        Only the job names are published, never the budgets or contacts in the Job Report. New jobs show up within two minutes, or press Refresh.
